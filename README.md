## The short version

I am **Nimesh Johari**—the AI Pod Tech Lead at Habuild. I lead a three-developer pod where backend engineering meets agentic AI: prompts, tools, memory, evaluations, observability, incidents, and the unglamorous engineering that makes all of them survive production.

I do not use AI as a substitute for understanding the code. I trace the full path, find the real owner, make the smallest safe change, and verify what the system actually stored or returned.

> **My default loop:** read the source → trace the runtime → reproduce the failure → fix the owner → verify the result.

## The workbench

Recent work includes taking **GPT-5.6 Luna** through production rollout, leading a **three-developer AI Pod**, and co-leading reliability work that reduced Langfuse/ClickHouse disk usage from **175 GiB to 12 GiB**.

Before the AI Pod, I worked in Habuild's CRM engineering pod on high-throughput messaging and failure recovery. Earlier, I led backend delivery for credit-card acquisition at **Freecharge** and built catalog, payment, order, onboarding, and chatbot systems at **BYJU'S**.
